Club History

Gachuru FC is a dynamic, community-driven football club built on a passion for the beautiful game and a commitment to developing local talent. Founded to unite players and fans through competitive sportsmanship, the club has grown into a dedicated squad focused on teamwork, discipline and pride on and off the pitch. Whether competing in local matches or welcoming new talent into the ranks, Gachuru FC continues to build its legacy one game at a time.
